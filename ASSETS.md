# Image sources

Driver portraits, team logos, and circuit artwork were supplied in `src/assets/Drivers`, `src/assets/Logo`, and `src/assets/maps`. Original files are preserved. The app imports resized WebP derivatives from `src/assets/optimized` and uses CSS to frame them without changing the originals.

The blue 2026 car render is the FIA concept image published with [the FIA's 2026 regulations announcement](https://www.fia.com/news/new-era-competition-fia-showcases-future-focused-formula-1-regulations-2026-and-beyond). [Original image](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/styles/panopoly_image_original/public/2026_car_render_2_9.jpg?itok=RdrzTjag). It is used in the intro and hero, with an attribution link on the dashboard. This concept artwork is independent of the app's 2024 archive statistics.

Bahrain did not have a matching supplied image, so its existing labelled illustrative circuit drawing remains. Only images that match existing archive entries are imported into the production build. Supplied photos and artwork can depict different years; their clothing, records, and embedded labels do not override the app's stated 2024 data.

The small F1 Pulse favicon is an original SVG mark. The configurable garage car remains the project's original SVG illustration.
